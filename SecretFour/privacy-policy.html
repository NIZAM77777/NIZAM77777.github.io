Privacy Policy

Secret Four by MN7Studios
Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MN7Studios ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play Secret Four (the "Game") on a mobile device. By installing or playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

  • Player name. The display name you choose is stored on your device and is shown to other players during online matches.
  • Game data. Your progress, settings, in-game items, and rewards are saved locally on your device.
  • Online play data. To connect you with other players, the Game signs you in anonymously through Unity Gaming Services. This creates a random anonymous player ID and processes technical data such as your IP address and device information needed to route the connection.
  • Advertising data. The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the Game and save your progress
  • To enable online multiplayer matches
  • To display ads, including rewarded ads that grant in-game rewards
  • To keep the Game stable and prevent abuse

3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) – Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps
  • Unity Gaming Services (anonymous sign-in and multiplayer relay) – Unity Privacy Policy

4. Advertising and Your Choices

Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID where the law allows it and, where required, only with your consent. In regions such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Game shows a consent message before personalized ads are served.

  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, where you can turn off tracking requests.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Your player name and game progress are stored on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it. To request deletion of data linked to your anonymous player ID, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
