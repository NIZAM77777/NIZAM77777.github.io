Privacy Policy - One Last Gambit

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android game One Last Gambit (package name: com.shalonrodrigs.onelastgambit).

The game is created by Shalon Rodrigs and published on Google Play by MN7 Studios ("we", "us").

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your player name, level, XP, achievements, unlocked weapons, ad-reward progress and settings on your device. We do not receive this information. You do not need an account to play. If device backup is turned on, Android may include this information in your own backup.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads with Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location based on your IP address, device information, interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. This is used to show and measure ads, improve the service and prevent fraud. Some ads are optional and give a reward in the game.

How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Your ad choices

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before showing ads. You can change your choice later with the Privacy button in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

4. Playing with friends

The Play With Friends mode uses Photon, a service of Exit Games, to connect players. Room codes, chess moves, weapon choices and other match information are sent through it. Photon may also process technical information such as your IP address. The game does not send your player name, and has no text or voice chat.

Photon Privacy Policy: https://www.photonengine.com/en-us/privacy

5. What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The game has no in-app purchases, and we do not sell the information saved by the game.

6. How long information is kept

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. Information processed by Google and Photon is kept according to their policies.

7. Deleting your information

To delete your game progress, clear the game's storage in your Android settings or uninstall the game, and delete any device backup. For information held by Google or Photon, use the controls in their policies. You can contact us for help.

8. Security

Our service providers use their own measures to protect information, but no method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

9. Children

One Last Gambit is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us.

10. International processing

Google and Photon may process information in countries other than yours.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Contact us to make a request.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will be on this page, with a new date.
