Four Fusion Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

MN7 Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Four Fusion.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Four Fusion.

By using Four Fusion, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Four Fusion does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, telephone number, or other directly identifying information to play the game.

However, Four Fusion uses third-party advertising services, including Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). These services may automatically collect certain information from your device and your interactions with advertisements and the application.

Depending on the applicable advertising configuration and device settings, information handled by the advertising SDK may include:

  • IP address and general location information derived from it.
  • Advertising or other device identifiers.
  • App and device information.
  • Information about interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Information about application launches, taps, and video views.

The exact information collected may depend on your device, operating system, advertising settings, consent choices, and the configuration of Google's advertising services.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled through third-party advertising services may be used for purposes including:

  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Personalizing advertisements where permitted.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Diagnosing and improving service performance.
  • Improving the reliability of the application.

MN7 Studios does not use Four Fusion to directly collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address for account creation.

3. Third-Party Advertising Services

Four Fusion uses Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google may collect and process information in accordance with Google's own privacy practices and policies.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

4. Advertising Choices

Depending on your location and device, you may have controls that allow you to manage advertising preferences or reset your advertising identifier.

You can also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device settings and Google's available privacy controls.

5. Children's Privacy

Four Fusion is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or similar personal information from children.

Because Four Fusion uses third-party advertising services, advertising and data practices may also be governed by the policies and controls of those services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application.

Information transmitted by Google's Mobile Ads SDK is protected using encryption in transit, as described by Google's documentation.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Four Fusion does not maintain a user account system or a developer-managed database containing personal user profiles.

Information collected by third-party advertising services may be retained and processed according to those providers' applicable policies and retention practices.

If you have questions about information processed by Google through advertising services, you should also review Google's privacy resources and controls.

8. Third-Party Services

Four Fusion may rely on third-party services to provide advertising and related functionality.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

The primary advertising service currently used by Four Fusion is:

  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Four Fusion, our services, applicable requirements, or third-party services.

When changes are made, the updated "Effective Date" at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.

10. Contact Us
