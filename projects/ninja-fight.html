IN DEVELOPMENT

Project Overview

Ninja Fight is a 2D action-combat game in development. Its visual direction follows a masked ninja hero through a stylized Asian-inspired world of temple rooftops, moonlit mountains, waterfalls and dangerous platform paths. The project is being shaped around fast-paced encounters and a distinctive ninja-fantasy atmosphere.

Game Direction

  • Ninja character concepts with multiple visual skin variations.
  • Weapon concepts including katana-style swords and ninja equipment.
  • Platform environments with ladders, moving platforms, breakable objects and hazards.
  • Temple structures, bridges, lanterns, banners and atmospheric world props.
  • Action-combat presentation designed for a responsive game experience.
