About Me

Who I Am

I'm Mohammed Nizam K N, a passionate Unity Game Developer, Gameplay Programmer, and the founder of MN7 Studios. I graduated with a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering, where I built a strong foundation in programming, software engineering, algorithms, and game development. I specialize in developing engaging 2D and 3D mobile games using Unity and C#, combining creativity with technical problem-solving to build polished and enjoyable gaming experiences.

What I Do

I independently develop Unity games from concept to completion, covering gameplay programming, game architecture, AI systems, UI development, level design, debugging, optimization, testing, and Android deployment. Every project helps me strengthen my understanding of clean code, reusable systems, and creating engaging player experiences.

My portfolio includes projects such as Mirror Escape, a unique puzzle game built around synchronized movement mechanics; Tic Tac Toe, featuring AI and local multiplayer; and Four Fusion, a strategic Connect Four game with multiple AI difficulty levels. Through these projects, I have gained practical experience in solving gameplay challenges while developing complete, production-ready mobile games using Unity.

Technologies & Development

Along with Unity and C#, I work with technologies including Git, GitHub, Visual Studio, Android Studio, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Flutter, and the Google Play Console. I enjoy learning new tools, experimenting with gameplay mechanics, and continuously improving both my technical and creative skills through hands-on development.

My Goal

As the founder of MN7 Studios, I am focused on developing and publishing original Unity games while expanding my portfolio and gaining real-world development experience. My long-term goal is to grow as a professional Unity Game Developer, contribute to innovative game development teams, and create high-quality games that combine engaging gameplay, technical excellence, and memorable player experiences. I am always eager to learn, take on new challenges, and continue building a successful career in the game development industry.
